Inside our workshop in Villacañas

Almost everything that leaves Áncora goes through this building. These photos are from an ordinary working day, nothing staged. This is the path an order follows, from a sheet of glass to the finished piece.

The building

Glass arrives in large sheets and is stored on racks along the building. The overhead crane moves the heaviest ones.

Cutting

Each piece is cut to size on the cutting table. Urgent jobs are cut on the spot; the rest are prepared without rushing.

Sandblasting

In this cabinet glass is decorated with sand under pressure. This is where the frosted patterns you see on many doors come from: flowers, borders, lettering.

Glazing doors

Villacañas is door country, and a good part of the daily work is glazing them: bottom bead, glass, silicone and top bead, in that order.

Leftover glass

Offcuts and broken pieces do not go in the bin. They are sorted into these containers for recycling.
