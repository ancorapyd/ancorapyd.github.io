Áncora · since 1980

A family tradition reflected in every cut

Áncora began in 1980, when Rafael Osuna opened his own workshop in Villacañas, after years of cutting glass in Madrid. Today his daughter runs Proyectos y Desarrollos Áncora and carries on that same tradition.
