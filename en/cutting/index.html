Cut glass, piece by piece

Glass cutting is the craft where it all began. Rafa Osuna started cutting glass at thirteen in Madrid. We no longer take cut-glass orders, but many of the pieces he made by hand are still at home. These are some of them.

The lathe

Rafa cut glass on a lathe. A wheel wears the glass away little by little while the cutter controls pressure, angle and speed. His lathes and wheels are still in the workshop.

"The tools are more or less the same: a lathe and a wheel. There are no miracles. Being a craft, the computer doesn't come into it. It's the lathe and the hands."
